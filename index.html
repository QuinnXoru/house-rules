WHY THESE RULES?

Let's keep the group chat helpful, not stressful.

These guidelines are meant to prevent misinformation, unnecessary notifications, and confusion while still giving students room to share legitimate school-related information.

01 — HOUSE RULES

The rules that keep us organized

01
ANNOUNCEMENTS

No Unnecessary Announcements

All announcements should go through a confirmation process with the officers. This helps us avoid the spread of misinformation.

Who may send formal announcements?

Only the officers should send formal announcements in the group chat.

What may students announce?

  • Class suspensions officially announced by the city government
  • Research survey questionnaires
  • Club activities
  • Announcements a professor directly instructed the student to share
Before posting: Any other formal announcement should go through the designated officer/admin first.
02
NOTIFICATIONS

Unnecessary Pings Are a Big No

Please avoid excessive use of @everyone, especially during late evenings. A ping can make people think there is an urgent school-related announcement when there isn't one.

Who may use @everyone?

Officers and block representatives may use the @everyone ping when it is genuinely necessary.

When is a ping appropriate?

Use it only for legitimate, relevant information that needs the attention of the group.

Think before you ping: If the message does not require everyone's attention, use a normal message or tag only the people who need to see it.
